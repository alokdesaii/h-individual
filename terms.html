Who can open an account

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must live in a country where we offer H Individual.
  • The account is for your personal use only, not for a business. For business payments, see H Business.
  • You can hold only one personal account.

Opening your account

The law requires us to confirm who you are before you can use your account. We'll ask for your personal details, a valid identity document, a selfie, and sometimes proof of address or information about where your money comes from.

You agree that the information you give us is true and up to date, and that you'll tell us if it changes. We can refuse to open an account, and we don't always have to give a reason.

Your balances

You can hold money in more than one currency. Money in your account is electronic money or a stored-value balance, not a bank deposit, so it is not covered by a deposit protection scheme. We keep customer money separate from our own money, as the rules require.

We don't pay interest on your balances.

Sending money

Before you confirm a transfer, we show you the amount you send, our fee, the exchange rate, the amount your recipient gets, and when we expect it to arrive. Once you confirm, we'll send the money as quickly as we can.

  • Check your recipient's details carefully. If you give us the wrong details, we'll try to help, but we may not be able to get the money back.
  • You can cancel a transfer only until we've sent the money on.
  • Arrival times are estimates. Delays can happen, for example because of checks we must carry out or because of the receiving bank or wallet.

Exchange rates and fees

We use the mid-market exchange rate and don't add a hidden margin to it. Instead we charge a clear fee, which we always show before you confirm. You can see our current fees on our Pricing page.

A quote is valid for a limited time. If it expires, we'll show you a new one before you confirm.

Digital assets

Digital assets such as Bitcoin, Ether, USDC and USDT can rise or fall in value quickly, and you could lose some or all of the money you put in. They are not money, not deposits and not protected by any compensation scheme.

Digital-asset services may not be available in every country. Before you use them, please read our Crypto risk notice.

Keeping your account safe

  • Keep your password, passcode and devices secure, and never share one-time codes with anyone, including people who say they work for us.
  • Tell us straight away if you think someone else has accessed your account or if your phone is lost or stolen.
  • If a payment is made from your account that you didn't authorise, tell us as soon as possible. We'll investigate and, where the rules say so, refund you.

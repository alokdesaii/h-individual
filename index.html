Help

Questions?

Can't find an answer? Email our team.

Which countries can I send to?

We are opening corridor by corridor, starting from Hong Kong and the UAE to India and the Philippines. Create an account and we'll tell you as soon as your route is live.

Is H Individual on web and mobile?

Yes. You can use H Individual in your browser and in our iPhone and Android apps. Your account and balances are the same everywhere.

How do you make money if you use the real rate?

We charge one clear fee, shown before you confirm. We never add a margin to the exchange rate.

Is there an account for businesses?

H Individual is for people sending and holding their own money. For companies, Harbour & Hills offers H Business.

What do I need to open an account?

You need to be 18 or over, with an email address, a phone number and a valid government ID such as a passport or HKID. Accounts are in your own name.

Can I send crypto to someone who only has a bank account?

Yes. Send in stablecoins and your recipient can receive local currency in their bank or mobile wallet.

Is crypto available everywhere?

No. Digital-asset services depend on local regulation and aren't offered in every country.
